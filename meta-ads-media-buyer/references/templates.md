## 1. Client intake questionnaire

**Business**
1. What do you sell, and to whom?
2. Prices, average order value, and gross margin (or product cost, shipping and fees)?
3. Where are your customers (countries, cities)? Which languages?
4. How do customers buy: website, form, call, WhatsApp or Messenger, app, in store? Is cash on delivery involved, and what share of orders is delivered or kept?
5. Do customers buy again? How often?

**Goals**
6. What result do you want from ads, and by when?
7. Target cost per sale or lead, or target return, if you have one?
8. Monthly ad budget, and is it fixed or can it grow with results?

**Current state**
9. Have you run Meta ads before? What worked, what did not? Recent monthly spend and results?
10. Is the pixel installed? Conversions API? Which events are tracked?
11. Any rejected ads or account restrictions?
12. Other channels in use (search, email, influencers, marketplaces)?

**Assets**
13. Website or landing page links. Product catalog?
14. Existing photos, videos, reviews, testimonials, creators you work with?
15. Customer email or phone list (collected with consent)?
16. Main competitors, and what makes you different?
17. Anything we must not say or show (legal, brand)?

## 2. Naming convention

Consistent names make reports readable and UTMs usable. Use underscores between fields and no spaces.

```
Campaign:  {Market}_{Objective}_{Funnel}_{Product/Offer}_{BudgetType}_{YYMM}
           EG_Sales_Prosp_SummerBundle_CBO_2606

Ad set:    {Audience}_{Geo}_{Age}_{Placement}_{OptimisationEvent}
           Broad_EG_25-54_AdvPlus_Purchase
           RT-ATC14d_EG_18-65_AdvPlus_Purchase

Ad:        {Format}_{Angle}_{Hook/Variant}_{Creator/Source}_{Date}
           VID_ProblemSolution_Hook2_Sara_260612
           IMG_Offer_20off_Design_260612
```

Common codes: Prosp (prospecting), RT (retargeting), RET (retention); VID, IMG, CAR (carousel), DPA (catalog); CBO or ABO.

## 6. Account audit report

```
# Meta Ads Audit: {Business} ({period reviewed})

## Summary
Overall health in two or three sentences. Top three priorities. Estimated impact where it can be argued.

## Scorecard
| Area | Status (Good / Needs work / Critical) | Main finding |
| Tracking and data quality |
| Account structure |
| Audiences |
| Creative |
| Bidding and budgets |
| Funnel and landing page |
| Measurement and reporting |
| Policy and account health |

## Findings by area
For each: what was observed (with numbers), why it matters, recommended fix.

## Action plan
| Priority | Action | Owner | Effort | Expected effect | How we will measure it |

## Quick wins (this week)
## Next 30 days
## Next 90 days
## Data that was missing
```

Audit checks, by area:
- **Tracking**: pixel plus CAPI, deduplication, Event Match Quality, value and currency, events versus real orders, UTMs.
- **Structure**: number of campaigns and ad sets versus conversion volume, ad sets stuck in learning limited, overlapping audiences, correct objectives and events.
- **Audiences**: reliance on narrow interests, exclusions, retargeting size and frequency, customer list freshness.
- **Creative**: number of distinct concepts live, format mix, age of top ads, hook rate and CTR spread, fatigue, launch cadence.
- **Bidding and budgets**: budget per ad set versus CPA, share of spend on winners, cap settings, pacing.
- **Funnel**: landing page view rate, conversion rate by step, mobile speed, ad-to-page match.
- **Measurement**: attribution settings, share of view-through, MER trend, new versus returning customers.
- **Account health**: rejected ads, restrictions, verification, admin access, payment issues.

## 8. Monthly client report

```
# {Month} report: {Business}

## Executive summary
Results against goals in plain language. Three bullets at most.

## Results
Spend, conversions, CPA, revenue, ROAS, MER, new customers; versus last month and target.

## Funnel
Impressions → clicks → landing page views → add to cart → purchases, with rates and where the main loss is.

## Creative insights
Top three ads with what made them work; concepts retired; themes customers respond to.

## Audience and placement insights

## Tests run and what we learned

## Next month
Priorities, planned tests, budget recommendation with reasoning, creative requests.

## Notes on the data
Attribution setting used, known tracking gaps, anything that makes periods not comparable.
```

Write client reports without jargon, lead with business outcomes, and be direct about bad results along with the plan to address them.
